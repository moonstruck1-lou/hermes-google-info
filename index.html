Personal use · Operated by Noah Samuels

A personal assistant for everyday Google Workspace tasks.

This private deployment of Hermes Agent helps its owner find information, summarize correspondence, and work with calendars and documents across authorized Google accounts.

What the integration does

Gmail
Search and read messages; send or organize mail when explicitly approved.
Calendar
Retrieve events; create, update, or delete entries when explicitly approved.
Drive
Find and read accessible files; upload, share, change, or delete files when explicitly approved.
Docs and Sheets
Read document text and spreadsheet values; create or edit content when explicitly approved.
Contacts
Read contact names, email addresses, and phone numbers for requested tasks.

How access works

The integration uses the official Nous Research Google Workspace skill and Google OAuth consent. Credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the operator’s Hermes environment. This website is informational: it does not collect Google sign-ins, authorization codes, tokens, or account content.

The granted Google permissions include write capabilities. The operator’s approval policy governs their use; it is not a technical read-only restriction. Retrieved content may be processed by the AI provider configured in Hermes. See the privacy policy for details.
