Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Hermes Google Workspace integration operated by Noah Samuels, including its use of Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts.

Data accessed and its purpose

With Google account authorization, Hermes can access email messages, attachments and metadata; calendar details and events; Drive file metadata and contents; document text; spreadsheet values; and contact names, email addresses and phone numbers. Data is used to perform requested personal-assistant tasks, such as searching, summarizing, preparing drafts, and checking schedules.

The installed skill requests Gmail read, send and modify permissions; Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets permissions that include write capabilities; and read-only Contacts permission. Sending mail or creating, modifying, sharing, or deleting Google data requires the operator’s explicit approval. Background monitoring is not enabled by this connection setup and requires separate authorization.

Processing and sharing

Hermes connects directly to Google using the operator’s OAuth client. Google authentication does not depend on the selected AI model provider. To perform a requested task, relevant retrieved content may be included in prompts and tool results sent to the AI provider configured in Hermes. If the operator uses a messaging interface, responses may also pass through that messaging service.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or made public by this integration. It must not be used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models. The operator must use AI-provider settings and services consistent with these restrictions before sending Google data to a provider. Providers may process and retain submitted content under their applicable terms and settings; this policy does not promise zero retention by those services.

Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and tokens are stored under the Hermes service account with restrictive filesystem permissions. Relevant Google content and generated responses may be retained in Hermes conversation history, task files, operational records, or backups in the operator’s environment. The operator controls access to that environment. No fixed automatic deletion period is claimed.

The public information pages do not connect to Google APIs, receive OAuth callbacks, or contain Google tokens or account content. They use no application analytics, cookies, or external scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard request logs, including IP addresses and browser metadata.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke the integration’s access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously stored content. Contact the operator to request deletion of locally stored tokens, conversation content, task files, and relevant backups, or to address copies held by configured service providers. Backup and provider retention may delay complete removal. Revoking access or deleting local copies does not itself delete original messages, events, or files in Google.

Google API Limited Use

Hermes Personal Assistant’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
